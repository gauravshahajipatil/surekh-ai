# SUREKH AI Implementation Phases

1. **Foundation:** repository, TypeScript UI/API, SQLite schema, authentication, role checks, project membership, audit baseline.
2. **Core product MVP:** projects, workspace, documents, client portal, reports, notifications, team/admin screens.
3. **Cost & finance:** estimates and server-authoritative BOQ calculations, CSV export, invoice records and status flow.
4. **Civil AI gateway:** optional Chat Completions adapter and explicitly marked demo fallback; no RAG grounding yet.
5. **Safety baseline:** event triage, camera inventory, feedback submission, intervention records and explainable rules-based risk.
6. **Research pipeline:** dataset versioning, annotations, baseline runners, metric storage and reproducibility manifests.
7. **Reliability:** image quality, lighting, occlusion, camera health, temporal consistency and OOD signals.
8. **Risk fusion & prediction:** multi-signal risk and future horizon prediction with uncertainty and lead-time evaluation.
9. **Near misses & intervention outcomes:** validated state machine, supervised recommendations and outcome measurement.
10. **Hardening:** tenant security tests, scanning, backups, recovery, observability, privacy assessment, dependency/security scans.
11. **Release:** staged deployment, monitoring, rollback, independently reviewed security controls and reproducible research package.
